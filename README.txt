# Kassem Hachem — Operations & Analytics Portfolio

Personal portfolio website for Kassem Hachem, an Operations & Analytics professional building Power BI dashboards, KPI systems, and AI-powered automations.

Live site: https://kassoom.github.io/

## Featured project

### GulfFix Operations Intelligence

A synthetic UAE home-services operations project that combines:

- Power BI executive and operations dashboards
- 11,348 completed jobs analysed from 12,000 source records
- AED 3.95M revenue and 76.5% overall SLA
- Deterministic KPI validation in n8n
- Local Llama analysis through LM Studio
- Evidence-backed management report generation

The case study includes screenshots of both Power BI pages and the complete n8n workflow.

## Site sections

- Professional background and measurable impact
- Contact and LinkedIn links

## Local preview

From the portfolio folder, run:

```text
npx --yes http-server . -p 4173
```

Then open http://localhost:4173/.
